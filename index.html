The big vision

One brand. One infrastructure backbone. Multiple income streams.

Instead of building ten disconnected businesses, AskMae AI is designed as one operating system where content, products, coaching, affiliate recommendations, courses, and data services reinforce one another over time.

Ecosystem layers

The AskMae AI machine

Each layer can launch independently, but the long-term value comes from how they share audience, data, AI workflows, and brand trust.

01

AskMae AI Core

The flagship AI operating system where visitors ask for wellness plans, budgets, side-hustle ideas, product comparisons, websites, and business plans.

02

Wellness & Wealth Hub

A paid membership for fitness plans, habit tracking, faith-based encouragement, budgeting, debt payoff, side hustles, journaling, and coaching.

03

Worth-It-2U Reviews

An affiliate content engine with product reviews, best-of lists, comparisons, and buying guides for Amazon Associates and future partner programs.

04

Digital Product Marketplace

Prompt packs, Notion and Canva templates, business planners, faith workbooks, wellness guides, and self-paced course assets that sell around the clock.

05

AskMae Academy

A course platform for AI basics, building an online business, AI automation, and premium training on launching an AI-powered brand.

06

AskMae Data Platform

A long-term DaaS layer that turns public business data, product intelligence, market trends, and AI-generated reports into dashboards, APIs, and insights.

07

AI Content Factory

Automated workflows for blogs, Pinterest pins, Facebook posts, email campaigns, product reviews, and news summaries that feed traffic and sales funnels.

08

Self-Hosted Infrastructure

A homelab and VPS backup running the website host, AI server, database, CRM, automation tools, digital store, and course platform under your control.

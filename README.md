# AskMae AI Ecosystem

AskMae AI is a self-hosted wellness, wealth, business, and data platform by Sadie. The project combines the AskMae AI operating system, AI Wellness & Wealth Hub, Worth-It-2U affiliate reviews, digital products, courses, coaching, a future DaaS platform, and local AI infrastructure into one ecosystem.

## Vision

The goal is to build one business machine instead of ten separate businesses. AskMae AI uses a shared brand, infrastructure backbone, AI workflow, audience, and data foundation so every revenue stream strengthens the next.

## Ecosystem Layers

1. **AskMae AI Core** — the flagship AI assistant for wellness, wealth, business planning, side hustles, product comparisons, and website help.
2. **AI Wellness & Wealth Hub** — a paid membership for fitness, habits, motivation, faith-based encouragement, budgeting, debt payoff, entrepreneurship, journaling, and coaching.
3. **Worth-It-2U Reviews** — an affiliate content engine for product reviews, best-of lists, buying guides, and comparisons.
4. **Digital Product Marketplace** — prompt packs, templates, planners, workbooks, and self-paced course assets.
5. **AskMae Academy** — a course platform for AI basics, online business, AI automation, and premium AI brand-building training.
6. **AskMae Data Platform** — a long-term DaaS platform for reports, dashboards, API access, market trends, product data, and consumer insights.
7. **AI Content Factory** — automated content workflows for blogs, social posts, email campaigns, product reviews, and summaries.
8. **Self-Hosted Infrastructure** — a homelab and VPS-backed stack for hosting, AI services, databases, automation, commerce, CRM, email, and courses.

## Running Locally

This repository currently ships as a static landing page. Serve it from the repository root with any static file server, for example:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000`.
